Main Content

Resize the browser to see the layout change:

  • < 600px: Header, Sidebar, Content all stacked.
  • 600–899px: Sidebar and Content side by side (30% / 70%).
  • ≥ 900px: Sidebar narrows (20% / 80%).

Floats + clearfix provide the basic two-column layout, and flexbox (via @supports) enhances it in modern browsers.
